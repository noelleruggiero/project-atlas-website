THE CHALLENGE

When people are displaced, educational histories can become fragmented, inaccessible, or lost. We believe that a change in geography should not erase a learner's progress.

01 / THE PLATFORM

Building continuity
across borders.

A digital platform designed to help displaced learners maintain control over their educational histories and access the tools they need to plan their next steps.

02 / RESEARCH & IMPACT

Beyond access.
Understanding outcomes.

Educational disruption does not end when a learner enters a new school or country. Understanding what happens next is essential to improving long-term outcomes.

Our longer-term vision is to support ethical, consent-based research examining how education, qualifications, and employment trajectories evolve across displacement contexts and national systems.

03 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Technology built
around the learner.

Educational information is personal. The technology should serve the people whose histories it holds.

01

Learner control

Individuals should retain control over their records and decisions about sharing them.

↗
02

Informed consent

Participation in research should be separate from access to essential platform services.

↗
03

Responsible research

Research should prioritize privacy, appropriate safeguards, and the responsible use of information.

↗
04

Equitable access

Essential tools and support should be designed with accessibility and inclusion in mind.
